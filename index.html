Model transparency

  • Assumes a higher IMDb rating = better quality and that using more of your free time is better; mood and taste aren't measured.
  • Limited to movies OMDb returns for your keyword (title search only, no genre filter), so genre is filtered after the fact; movies missing runtime/rating are skipped.
  • Score (0–100) = weighted average of Quality (rating ÷ runtime, scaled against the best in these results) and Time Fit (runtime ÷ your max). It's a relative match to your weights, not a guarantee you'll enjoy it.
